PEK TV
A media center that is simple for regular people and deep for power users: your own library, plugins you can trust, and a screen made for the TV.
Docs: https://r3ct01.github.io/PEK-TV/  ·  Status: beta
What it does
Your library, everywhere. Local folders and network shares (SMB, NFS, FTP, WebDAV, UPnP/DLNA) with discovery. Reads Kodi-style NFO files and artwork first, and asks TMDb only for what is missing.
A player that stays out of the way. Automatic subtitles, Skip Intro / Skip Recap, and an Up Next card near the end of an episode.
Built for the couch. D-pad, remote and gamepad support, a 10-foot TV mode, and a performance mode for weak devices.
A dashboard you design. Clock, Weather, Continue Watching, Recently Added, Favorites and plugin shortcuts.
Profiles and PEK Cloud. Netflix-style profiles; optional sign-in syncs history, resume points, favorites and settings.
Skins. Install a skin from a .zip. Ships with PEK Ember (default), Green and Neon.
Sandboxed plugins. Plugins declare their permissions and run in a sandbox. Three tiers: Official, the PEK Store, and install-from-GitHub (marked unverified).
Live TV through plugins. Channel switcher, guide and now / next banner, fed by a plugin.
